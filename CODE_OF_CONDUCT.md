# Community code of conduct

FindMyStuff welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Explain technical disagreements with evidence and focus on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not publish household photos, inventories, precise storage locations or credentials belonging to someone else.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect contributors. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently.

For a private concern, use a maintainer contact published on their GitHub profile. A dedicated private reporting channel has not been configured yet. Do not put confidential reports in a public issue; contact the maintainer first to agree on a private channel.
